Focus areas

Where leaders most need clear, current information

Our community reports follow the HHS Healthy People 2030 social determinants of health framework so priorities connect to measurable local needs—inside a product built for daily leadership use. Materials are for planning and situational awareness only.

Health equity

Where residents face barriers in income, education, care access, neighborhood, and community life.

Cancer detection and prevention

Screening gaps, follow-up pathways, and outreach priorities for populations most at risk—using public benchmarks and local context.

Aging in place and disability access

Home modification, mobility, accessibility, and isolation priorities for older residents.

Language access and inclusion

Plans and materials that support reaching residents in the languages they use, consistent with Title VI expectations for covered entities.

Housing and transportation

Cost burdens, housing age, transit, and rides that affect health and access to services.

Compliance and accountability

Clear owners, deadlines, and status for requirements your office tracks. Not a substitute for legal counsel.
